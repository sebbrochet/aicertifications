Prefer to read offline? Grab the latest build of the book. These files are regenerated automatically
from the current sources on every update, so they always match the web version you are reading.

- **PDF** — best for printing and desktop reading (built with XeLaTeX; diagrams rendered to images).
- **EPUB** — best for e-readers and phones (reflowable text, embedded cover).
- **Kindle (AZW3)** — native Kindle format for Paperwhite and other devices. Send it to your Kindle
  (email-to-Kindle or USB); the callouts use bordered boxes with monochrome icons instead of emoji
  so they render cleanly on Kindle's fonts.

> 💡 **Tip**: on the exam-readiness chapters, the mock-exam answers are collapsible in the web version
> and shown inline in the PDF, EPUB, and Kindle editions — handy for self-testing on paper or an e-reader.
